# Implementation Planner

## Instructions

### Step 1: Restate the objective
- Summarize the requested outcome in one or two sentences.
- Identify constraints, dependencies, and any assumptions that affect execution.

### Step 2: Break work into milestones
- Split the task into small, ordered phases.
- Keep each phase actionable and limited in scope.
- Separate discovery, implementation, verification, and cleanup work.

### Step 3: Sequence the work
- Order steps to reduce risk and dependency conflicts.
- Highlight what must happen first, what can run in parallel, and what should wait.
- Call out blockers or missing inputs early.

### Step 4: Make the plan practical
- Include the files, modules, commands, or artifacts likely to change.
- Keep the plan concrete enough that it can be followed immediately.
- Avoid overplanning; include only the steps needed to reach the goal.

### Step 5: Verify completion
- Define how success will be checked.
- Include tests, builds, manual checks, or acceptance criteria when relevant.
- Ensure the final step leaves the system in a usable state.
